# REWARDS

> Winning should have consequences.
>
> Preferably positive ones.

The reward system exists to turn competition into something meaningful.

A leaderboard without rewards creates status.

A leaderboard with rewards creates competition.

The arena is built around both.

---

# 💰 WHY REWARDS EXIST

The objective is simple:

Play well.

Rank well.

Win.

The reward system exists to recognize the best performer in a completed tournament round.

Not participation.

Not effort.

Not intentions.

Performance.

---

# 🏆 WHO GETS REWARDED?

The eligible winner of a tournament becomes eligible for the corresponding tournament reward.

A tournament produces:

## One Champion

The champion sits at the top of the leaderboard when:

- The tournament ends
- Scores are validated
- Rankings are finalized

That player becomes the round's winner.

---

# ⚡ HOW REWARDS ARE FUNDED

The arena is designed around a simple principle:

> The arena feeds the arena.

Protocol activity generates fees.

Part of the ecosystem is designed to support competition.

That creates a loop:

```text
ACTIVITY
    ↓
 FEES
    ↓
 REWARDS
    ↓
 COMPETITION
    ↓
 MORE ACTIVITY
```

The healthier the ecosystem becomes...

the stronger the reward engine can become.

---

# 🏦 THE REWARD VAULT

Rewards are supported through dedicated reward infrastructure.

The purpose of the reward vault is simple:

Store resources intended for competition.

Support tournament payouts.

Maintain transparency.

Provide verifiable reward activity.

---

# 🔎 PUBLIC VERIFICATION

Wraith can announce a reward.

The blockchain should verify it.

A healthy reward system should be:

- Transparent
- Traceable
- Verifiable
- Auditable

The objective is simple:

If somebody wins a tournament...

there should be evidence.

Not promises.

Receipts.

---

# 📈 REWARD GROWTH

The reward system is designed to be connected to ecosystem activity.

More participation can create:

- More activity
- More fees
- Larger reward pools
- Larger competitive incentives

Which then encourages more participation.

That feedback loop is intentional.

---

# 🧾 REWARD PROCESSING

A typical reward flow looks like:

```text
TOURNAMENT ENDS
        ↓
WINNER VERIFIED
        ↓
REWARD ELIGIBILITY CONFIRMED
        ↓
REWARD PROCESSED
        ↓
RECORD CREATED
        ↓
WRAITH TALKS ABOUT IT
```

The important thing:

The winner is confirmed before rewards are processed.

Not after.

---

# ⚖️ FAIRNESS

Rewards depend on competitive performance.

Not:

- Popularity
- Social status
- Wallet size
- Personal relationships

The objective is simple:

The player that earns the winning score should be the player that receives the reward.

Anything else breaks the arena.

---

# 📜 REWARD HISTORY

Every completed reward creates history.

Over time the arena can develop:

- Previous champions
- Historical payouts
- Winning streaks
- Legendary performances
- Rivalries

History matters.

Every reward leaves a footprint.

---

# 🚫 WHAT REWARDS ARE NOT

Rewards are not:

- Guaranteed income
- Yield generation
- Interest payments
- Employment
- A promise of profit

Rewards are competitive outcomes associated with tournament participation.

Winning is not guaranteed.

In fact, the entire point is that most players won't win.
